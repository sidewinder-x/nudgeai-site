Features

Built for the moment
the deal is won or lost.

Every feature is designed for one thing: giving you the right word at the right second.

🎯

Real-time objection handling

Detects price, trust, urgency, authority, competitor, and fear-of-change objections the moment the client says them.

Talk ratio monitor
