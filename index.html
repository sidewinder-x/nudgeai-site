Live Assist

A coach that stays out of the room.

The rep sees one precise suggestion. The buyer never sees Nudge. The call keeps its natural rhythm while the hard moments become easier to handle.

Designed for the moment between hearing and replying.

Short hints appear as a soft overlay, then fade before they become noise.

01 / Listen

Capture both sides of the call.

Use your microphone for the seller and system audio for the customer across Zoom, Meet, WhatsApp, or recorded practice calls.

02 / Decide

Filter the moments worth interrupting.

The decision engine weighs stage, recency, category, and urgency so the coach stays calm until it matters.

03 / Nudge

Show one move, not a lecture.

Every hint is direct, short, and actionable: ask for criteria, pause, reframe value, or lock a date and time.
